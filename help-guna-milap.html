What This Tool Does

Guna Milap is a pre-screening compatibility calculator (also called the Alliance Filter). Given one fixed person's birth Nakshatra, Rashi and Pada, it computes the ranked list of partner birth combinations that score best on the 36-guna Ashtakoota — without you having to manually enter each candidate pair.

This is an inverse lookup: instead of testing pair-by-pair (which is what Vivaha Muhurta does), you define yourself as the anchor and the tool finds which partner nakshatra/rashi/pada combinations are astrologically compatible with you.

Use case: A match coordinator or parent who has N biodata profiles. Run Guna Milap once to get a whitelist of compatible birth combinations, then keep only those profiles whose birth data lands on a whitelist row. Then verify the shortlisted couples through Vivaha Muhurta for full date scanning.

How the Ranking Works

The engine generates all 108 canonical Moon-pada positions (27 nakshatras × 4 padas), folded into 36 valid (nakshatra, rashi) pairs. For each pair, it finds the best achievable score over valid padas (pada-agnostic — you do not need to choose a pada upfront; the tool tells you the recommended one).

Rows are ranked by:

  1. Compatible first — Stage 1 pass (score ≥ 18, no open Nadi/Bhakoot dosha)
  2. Total score descending — highest Gunas first
  3. Tier label — Most Excellent > Excellent > Medium > Not Suitable
  4. Parihara count — Nadi/Bhakoot cancelled is better than open
  5. Lower dosha severity

Tier Labels

  • MOST_EXCELLENT — 31–36 Gunas. Exceptionally strong compatibility.
  • EXCELLENT — 26–30 Gunas. Very good match.
  • MEDIUM — 18–25 Gunas. Passes classical minimum. Acceptable.
  • NOT_SUITABLE — Below 18 or uncanceled Nadi/Bhakoot dosha. Does not qualify.

The Koota Breakdown

Each row's score can be expanded to see all 8 Kootas: Varna (1), Vashya (2), Tara (3), Yoni (4), Maitri (5), Gana (6), Bhakoot (7), Nadi (8). Nadi and Bhakoot rows also show whether a Parihara (classical cancellation) was applied and the reason.

Typical Workflow

  1. Enter your own Nakshatra, Rashi and Pada
  2. Click Compute Best Matches
  3. Review the Compatible Whitelist — all rows that pass Stage 1 eligibility
  4. Cross-reference your candidate biodatas against the whitelist table
  5. For promising pairs, open Vivaha Muhurta to run the full 2-stage check + date scan
Note: Guna Milap does not scan calendar dates. It is a birth-combination pre-filter. Once you shortlist a candidate, move to Vivaha Muhurta for the full marriage date search.

Limitations

  • This tool evaluates only Nakshatra, Rashi and Pada — not the full birth chart.
  • The "best pada" is a computational optimum over valid quarters. If the actual partner has a different pada, the score may differ.
  • Ashtakoota is one system among several classical matchmaking traditions.
  • Use this as a shortlisting aid, not a final verdict. Always consult a qualified jyotishi.
  • All computation is local — no data leaves your browser.
