In the digital age, electional astrology (*Muhurta*) has evolved beyond classical agricultural and agrarian milestones to encompass modern technological, corporate, and digital workflows. Modern astrologers and software engines map these contemporary events back to classical functional groups (*Nakshatra temperaments*, *Tithis*, and *Lagnas*) based on the underlying nature of the digital action.

Below is a structured list of modern *Muhurtas* commonly computed in digital and software applications today:

---

### 1. Software Engineering & Product Development

* **Repository Initialization / Project Creation (`ACT_DEV_INIT_PROJECT`)**
* **Nature:** Laying a foundational structure intended for long-term growth and stability.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Sthira / Fixed** (*Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttarashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada*).


* **Goal:** Ensures project longevity, clean architecture, and sustained team contribution.


* **First Production Release / Deployment (`ACT_DEV_PROD_DEPLOY`)**
* **Nature:** Public exposure, execution, swift movement, and visibility.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Kshipra / Laghu (Swift)** (*Hasta, Ashwini, Pushya*) or **Chara / Movable** (*Swati, Punarvasu, Shravana, Dhanishtha, Shatabhisha*).


* **Goal:** Smooth deployments, minimal downtime, and low bug rates post-launch.


* **Critical Bug Fix / Patch Deployment (`ACT_DEV_HOTFIX`)**
* **Nature:** Rapid, sharp intervention to eliminate a crisis.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Tikshna / Ugra (Sharp/Cruel)** (*Ardra, Jyeshtha, Moola, Purva Phalguni*).


* **Goal:** Effective resolution of security vulnerabilities or major server issues.


* **Database Migration / Infrastructure Overhaul (`ACT_DEV_DB_MIGRATION`)**
* **Nature:** Deep structural shifts, excavation, and data storage.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Adhomukhi (Downward-facing)** Nakshatras and **Sthira** elements.


* **Goal:** Prevents data corruption, data loss, and infrastructure downtime.



---

### 2. Digital Product & Web Launches

* **Website Domain Registration & Launch (`ACT_WEB_DOMAIN_LAUNCH`)**
* **Nature:** Establishing identity, global connectivity, and commerce.


* **Classical Mapping:** **Kshipra / Laghu** and **Tiryakamukhi (Sideways-facing)** for trade and network communications.


* **Goal:** High web traffic, site uptime, and protection against cybersecurity threats.


* **Mobile App Store / Play Store Submission (`ACT_APP_STORE_SUBMIT`)**
* **Nature:** Public outreach, trade, and user adoption.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Chara (Movable)** paired with **Purna / Nanda Tithis**.


* **Goal:** Smooth store approvals, positive user reviews, and viral organic reach.


* **SaaS Subscription / Cloud Infrastructure Provisioning (`ACT_CLOUD_PROVISION`)**
* **Nature:** Recurring resource usage, financial commitments, and contract binding.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Chara** (for scalability) or **Sthira** (for fixed long-term servers).





---

### 3. Digital Business, Finance & E-Commerce

* **E-Commerce Storefront Opening (`ACT_BIZ_ECOMM_OPEN`)**
* **Nature:** Commercial trade, buying, selling, and customer acquisition.


* **Classical Mapping:** **Kshipra (Swift)** and **Mridu (Tender)** (*Mrigashira, Chitra, Anuradha, Revati*).


* **Goal:** High conversion rates, steady revenue flow, and good customer feedback.


* **Stripe / Payment Gateway Integration & Go-Live (`ACT_FIN_PAYMENT_GOLIVE`)**
* **Nature:** Financial transactions, fund flows, and security.


* **Classical Mapping:** **Pushya** or **Hasta** Nakshatras, strictly avoiding *Rikta Tithis* and *Rahu Kalam*.


* **Goal:** Uninterrupted payment processing and low chargeback/fraud rates.


* **Cryptocurrency / Digital Asset Staking & Investment (`ACT_FIN_CRYPTO_STAKE`)**
* **Nature:** Asset locking, yield generation, and wealth accumulation.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Sthira (Fixed)** Nakshatras, or specific **Gulika Kalam** windows (which promote repetition and accumulation of wealth).





---

### 4. Marketing, Content & Social Media

* **Social Media Channel / Brand Launch (`ACT_MKT_BRAND_LAUNCH`)**
* **Nature:** Communication, public relations, and viral growth.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Mridu (Tender)** and **Chara (Movable)** Nakshatras.


* **Goal:** Strong follower growth, brand reputation, and audience engagement.


* **Ad Campaign Go-Live / Product Hunt Launch (`ACT_MKT_CAMPAIGN_START`)**
* **Nature:** Swift execution, competition, and immediate impact.
* **Classical Mapping:** **Kshipra / Laghu** (*Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta*) during **Abhijit Muhurta**.


* **Goal:** Maximum return on ad spend (ROAS) and campaign performance.


* **Publishing Digital Media / E-Book / Online Course (`ACT_PUB_DIGITAL_CONTENT`)**
* **Nature:** Education, distribution of knowledge, and arts (*Vidyarambha*).


* **Classical Mapping:** **Gauri / Saraswati** alignments, **Brahma Muhurta**, or **Purna Tithis**.
