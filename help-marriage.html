What This Tool Does

This tool performs two-stage marriage eligibility per Muhurta Chintamani, Chapter 6 (Vivaha Prakarana):

  1. Stage 1 — Ashtakoota (36-guna) Matchmaking: Compares the Nakshatra, Rashi and Pada of the Groom and Bride across 8 Kootas totalling 36 points. Checks for classical cancelling conditions (Parihara) if Nadi or Bhakoot score zero.
  2. Stage 2 — Wedding Date Search: If the couple is compatible, scans the target calendar window for Shubh Vivaha dates — filtering through universal Hard Blockers (combust Jupiter/Venus, Bhadra, Vivaha Doshas) and dual personal filters (Tara Bala & Chandra Bala for both partners).

The 8 Kootas (Ashtakoota)

Each Koota tests a different dimension of compatibility. The total score is out of 36 Gunas:

The Hard Blockers

Nadi Dosha

If both partners share the same Nadi (Adi, Madhya or Antya), the Nadi score drops to 0 and the match is blocked unless one of these classical Pariharas applies:

  • Same Rashi, different Nakshatras
  • Same Nakshatra, different Rashis (spanning a Rashi boundary)
  • Same Nakshatra, different Padas (quarter distinction)
  • Rashi Lord friendship — the lords of both Moon signs are friends

If canceled, the full 8 Nadi points are restored. If uncanceled, the match is hard blocked regardless of total score.

Bhakoot Dosha

Occurs when the couple's Rashis are 2-12, 5-9, or 6-8 positions apart. Cancelled if the Rashi lords are identical or mutual friends. If uncanceled, the match is hard blocked.

Important: A hard-blocked match means wedding dates cannot be generated for this combination per classical guidelines. The tool will show a clear message explaining why.

Eligibility Thresholds

To proceed to Stage 2 (date search), the couple must satisfy both conditions:

  1. PASS No uncanceled Nadi or Bhakoot Dosha
  2. PASS Total Guna score ≥ 18 / 36

Scores 25+ are considered strong matches. Scores 18–24 are borderline but eligible.

Stage 2: Wedding Date Search

Once the couple passes Stage 1, click Find Shubh Dates to scan the target window. The engine checks:

Universal Marriage Hard Blockers

  • Asta Guru / Asta Shukra — Jupiter or Venus combust (absolute zero tolerance)
  • Bhadra in Mrityu Loka — active Bhadra on Earth
  • 4 Major Vivaha Doshas — Latta (planetary kick), Jamitra (7th house malefic), Pata (Vyatipata/Vaidhriti yoga), Ekargala (solar-lunar imbalance)
  • Month eligibility — Sun-sign region masks (Aadi, Margazhi, etc. excluded per regional practice)

Dual Personal Filters

Tara Bala and Chandra Bala are evaluated for both the Groom and Bride. If either partner suffers Naidhana Tara (7th star) or Ashtama Chandra (Moon in 8th from birth sign), that date is rejected.

Scoring

Each surviving slot receives a quality score out of 100. Slots with score ≥ 80 are labelled Shubh, 65–79 are Neutral, and below 65 are Ashubh. The top-ranked Shubh dates and their time windows are displayed.

Inputs Explained

FieldDescription

From / ToThe calendar window to search for wedding dates. Defaults to current month + 6 months ahead.
RegionSun-sign month eligibility mask per regional practice. Controls which solar months are considered eligible for marriage.

Recommended Workflow

  1. Enter both partners' details — Nakshatra, Rashi, Pada
  2. Click "Calculate Compatibility" — reviews the 36-guna breakdown, dosha status and parihara details
  3. If eligible — click "Find Shubh Dates" to scan the target window for wedding dates
  4. Review the results — ranked Shubh dates with time windows, quality badges, and classical provenance
Tip: If the match fails Stage 1, the classical verse citation is shown so you can understand why. This is not a final verdict — consult a qualified jyotishi for a complete chart analysis.

Limitations

  • This tool uses only Nakshatra, Rashi and Pada — it does not evaluate full birth charts (planet positions, houses, aspects).
  • Ashtakoota is one of several classical matchmaking systems. Some traditions weigh the 8 Kootas differently.
  • Panchanga values, ayanamsa (Lahiri), and month rules vary by school. Always confirm with a trusted panchanga.
  • For the actual wedding ceremony, consult a qualified jyotishi for a complete Muhurta including Lagna selection.
  • All computation runs locally in your browser via the Swiss Ephemeris — no data leaves your machine.
