What This Calendar Does

This dashboard is a daily Panchang calendar — not a muhurta finder. It shows your personal lunar calendar for the current month, computed at sunrise from your birth star and location. Every card is generated locally in your browser with the Swiss Ephemeris (WASM, Lahiri ayanamsa); nothing is sent to a server.

Shubh date search for 140 activities now lives separately: 🕰 Muhurta — Shubh Date Finder. Birth star and month are shared between the two pages via local storage.

Order on the page is fixed: Key Lunar Days → Avoid Days → Daily Panchang → Daily Nitya → Verse of the Day → 7-day Pulse → Today's Gochara Pulse → Monthly Gochara → Month table. Avoid Days stays at the top (hard-avoid layer).

Step 1: Set Your Birth Data

In the landing card, pick your Janma Nakshatra (birth star, 27). Your Janma Rashi is auto-filled from the nakshatra; correct it if your star spans two rashis. Use City quick-select (382 cities) to autofill place, lat/lon and timezone — search “Madurai” etc., pick a city chip (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem) or type in the combo. Manual lat/lon/tz stay editable as advanced override. Click Compute calendar.

Tip: If you don't know your nakshatra, use your Moon sign (Rashi) as an approximation and confirm with a jyotishi later. Location is used for sunrise/sunset, Tamil month, and Tamil Nadu-only festival/holiday chips.

Key Lunar Days — Amavasya · Purnima · Janma

Right before Avoid Days, three pills show this month's anchors:

  • Amavasya ● Amavasya — new-moon tithi day
  • Purnima ○ Purnima — full-moon tithi day
  • Janma Nakshatra ✦ Janma — transit Moon in your birth star (1–2 dates per civil month, e.g. Ashwini → Sep 01 & 29)

Tap a pill to select that date in the month table below and scroll to its expanded detail. Dates are sunrise-based. Loading is automatic — the month table loads when you scroll near it (on-demand for fast LCP).

Avoid Days — Ashtama Chandra (Chandrashtama)

The first hard-avoid layer. Moon in your 8th rashi (~2.25 d, Coarse) and its peak nakshatra (~24 h, Peak) are flagged as avoid important initiations other than routine. Shown at the very top, never diluted by Gochara. The two-tone bar mirrors the calendar chips.

Daily Panchang Digest — Today — Panchang

Capsule of engine.js:computeDay at sunrise: Tithi (lunar day + paksha), Nakshatra (moon mansion), Yoga, Karana, sunrise/sunset, Rahu/Yama/Gulika start times, Abhijit (~48 min around true solar noon), and today's Chandrashtama/Tara status. No birth → Chandrashtama/Tara show “needs janma”.

Daily Nitya Sadhana — Today — Nitya

Lifts the hidden Tithi Nitya from the month table into a hero card. Based on Tantraraja 16 kalās (15 tithi Nityas + Mahā Nityā Lalitā on Amavasya/Purnima). Shows Devi name, kalā · bīja · paksha, dhyāna link, and the full tarpaṇa vidyā with Copy behind a subtle dīkṣā notice. This is the same Nitya shown inline as Pratipad · Kāmeśvarī in the month's Tithi column.

Tirumandiram — Verse of the Day

Daily rotation dayOfYear % 365 from Thirumoolar's 3047 verses (`rules/tirumandiram_daily.json`). Shows Tamil verse (pre-wrap), transliteration, English, and Read on tirumandiram.in ↗ + Copy/Prev/Next. No ayanamsa, purely calendrical.

Upcoming 7-day Pulse — Next 7 days

Weekly planning strip: next 7 days scanned from the month's dayMap+7 — Pradosham (Trayodashi), Ekadashi, Amavasya/Purnima, Tamil festivals, Siddhar/Mahaan pujas, with Nitya tag + chips. Uses the same tithi/tamilDate/siddhar matchers as the month table.

Today's Gochara Pulse — Daily Moon Chandrabala + Tārā

Daily relevance from monthly Gochara: today's transit Moon house from janma rāśi (1,3,6,7,10,11 favourable green; 4,12 amber; 8th Ashtama red) and Tara (Sampat/Kṣema/Sādhaka/Mitra/Parama-Mitra ✨ vs Vipat/Pratyari/Vadha ⚠️). Vedha-aware (⚑). Times till next rashi/nakshatra before next sunrise shown as till 07:26 → 9th dual chips. Needs birth; without it shows only transit Moon rāśi/nakshatra.

Gochara — Monthly Transits

Sidereal (Lahiri) rashi ingresses for the month (Moon excluded, 5–7 ingresses). For each: date (IST), planet From→To, house from janma, śubha/madhyama/aśubha chip, Tara badge, one-line classical paraphrase + verse cite. Impersonal list shown without birth. Classical basis Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra Ch.30–32.

The Monthly Calendar

On-demand table (loads when scrolled into view, or via Load Panchang). Columns: Date · Tithi (with Nitya inline) · Nakshatra · Kalam (Rahu/Yama/Gulika start times) · Events.

Events Chips

  • Amavasya / Purnima — tithi phase
  • Chandrashtama / Peak — 8th rashi + peak nakshatra
  • Siddhar — Guru Pooja / Jayanthi (purple)
  • Festival — Tamil festivals (gold)
  • Holiday — TN holidays (teal, TN-only)
  • Nitya — Tithi Nitya inline
  • Sankranti — Tamil month 1st
  • Eclipse — solar/lunar

Row Details (Tap a Date)

Tap any date row to expand its day detail card inline (sunrise/sunset, Kalam strips, Tamil date, all windows and festival periods, plus the full Nitya block with mantra Copy). Tap again to collapse. Copy events copies the expanded detail; Print / PDF and Download ICS export the visible month.

City quick-select: above the birth form; 5 preset chips + searchable combo (Madurai etc.) autofill bPlace/bLat/bLon/bTz. Manual fields stay editable.

Night Mode

Moon icon in the header toggles dark theme. In night mode Amavasya/Purnima chips use #34365E on light text for contrast, and all cards/tables switch to dark surfaces.

Classical Sources

Panchang: local sunrise + Lahiri ayanamsa. Avoid Days: Muhurta Chintamani Tara/Chandra logic. Nitya: Tantraraja / Jñānārṇava 16 kalās. Gochara: BPHS Ch.30–32 + Sārāvalī. Tamil festivals: localised calendar. All verses in provenance registry carry proof==="confirmed".

Limitations

  • Personal aid, not a substitute for a qualified jyotishi.
  • Panchanga values vary by school, ayanamsa and location; classifications may differ from your family pundit.
  • For dates that matter — marriage, travel, purchases, ceremonies — confirm against a trusted panchang.
  • All computation uses Lahiri ayanamsa and Swiss Ephemeris.
