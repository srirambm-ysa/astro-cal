What This Calendar Does

This tool computes daily Vedic muhurta windows — mathematically determined auspicious time slots — for any activity, filtered through your birth star (Janma Nakshatra). It follows the rules of Muhurta Chintamani (Ch. 2–3) and the classical Panchanga tradition.

All computation runs locally in your browser using the Swiss Ephemeris in WebAssembly — no data is sent to any server.

Step 1: Set Your Birth Data

In the landing card, select your Janma Nakshatra (birth star from 1 to 27). Your Janma Rashi is auto-filled from the nakshatra; you may correct it for stars that span two rashis. Enter your location (place name or lat/lon) for accurate sunrise & sunset timings. Click Compute calendar.

Tip: If you do not know your nakshatra, consult your family records or a qualified jyotishi. The closest approximation is your Moon sign (Rashi) — 12 rashis map to 27 nakshatras.

Step 2: Select Domain → Activity → Sub-activity

Every muhurta is activity-specific — there is no "blanket" auspicious time. Use the three-level cascade:

  • Domain — broad category (e.g. Real Estate, Marriage, Vehicle, Ceremony)
  • Activity — specific type (e.g. Griha Pravesha, Vehicle Purchase, Marriage)
  • Sub-activity / Task — the exact task (e.g. New Home, Used Home, Renovation)

Each sub-activity has its own rulebook — Hard Blockers, preferred nakshatras, tithi groups, and functional group (Sthira / Chara / Mridu / Kshipra / Tikshna). The engine picks the correct rules automatically.

Tip: Use the Quick Picks chips above the dropdowns for frequent combinations. Click a chip to set all three levels instantly. Click + Save to pin your current selection as a new chip.

Step 3: Choose a Mode

The mode selects how strictly the activity's rulebook is applied:

ModeUses birth star?Score cutoffWhen to use
Full muhurta❌ No≥ 80 (Shubh) / 65–79 (Madhyama)Important ceremonies, multi-stakeholder events. Strictest window.
Soft❌ No≥ 60Urgent tasks, tight deadlines, everyday activities. Relaxed criteria.
Personal days✅ Tara + Chandra≥ 75 (must first pass universal ≥ 60)Sole-actor activities. Adds Tara Bala & Chandra Bala from your birth chart.

Full and Soft are impersonal — they evaluate only the universal time quality for the selected activity. Personal days first checks the slot passes universal rules, then applies your personal Tara Bala (transit nakshatra vs your birth nakshatra) and Chandra Bala (moon transit vs your birth rashi). If either is severely afflicted (Naidhana Tara / Ashtama Chandra), the slot is rejected regardless of the universal score.

Step 4: Read the Results

Muhurta Table

The table shows every day in the current month that passes all Hard Blockers for the selected activity + mode. Columns:

  • Date — Gregorian date
  • Tara — Tara Bala position (1–9) relative to your birth star. Shown for reference in all modes; only used for scoring in Personal mode.
  • Nakshatra — the ruling lunar mansion for that day
  • Verdict — quality badge: Shubh (good window), Neutral (acceptable), Ashubh (not recommended)

Verdict Colour Key

  • Shubh — Teal green. The slot meets all criteria with room to spare. Preferred for important tasks.
  • Neutral — Gold / saffron. The slot passes but is borderline. Acceptable for routine or less critical work.
  • Ashubh — Vermilion red. The slot failed one or more conditions. Skip if possible.

Row Details (Click a Shubh day)

Click any Shubh row to expand the full muhurta detail panel — you will see:

  • Universal score (0–100) and tier breakdown (T1 Hard Blockers, T2 Primary, T3 Secondary)
  • Personal metrics (Tara name & bonus, Chandra house & bonus) — only in Personal mode
  • Time windows — sunrise/sunset, Abhijit window, specific muhurta timings
  • Classical provenance — click the Source button to see the exact Muhurta Chintamani verse citations behind every rule

The Monthly Calendar

Below the muhurta results, a full month table shows every day with its Tithi (lunar day), Nakshatra, Kalam (Rahu, Yama, Gulika start times), and Events (festivals, sankranti, moon phases, eclipses). Click any row to expand the day's full detail — sunrise/sunset, muhurta windows, and festival periods.

Tip: Use the Print / PDF button for a clean printable version. The Download ICS button exports event data to a calendar file.

Quick Picks & Presets

The Quick Picks row above the dropdowns stores your frequently used activity combinations as clickable chips. Click a chip to instantly set domain, activity, and sub-activity. Click + Save to create a new chip from your current dropdown selection. Remove a saved chip by clicking the × on it.

Night Mode

Toggle between light and dark theme using the moon icon button in the header. This affects all cards, tables, and the frame border — chosen colours are adjusted for comfortable low-light reading.

Classical Sources

Every scoring rule, Hard Blocker, and override in this engine is drawn from Muhurta Chintamani by Acharya Rama Daivagya. Click the Source button (book icon) next to the activity name to view the exact Sanskrit verse, chapter reference, and English rationale for the current activity's rules.

Limitations

  • This tool is a personal muhurta aid, not a substitute for professional jyotisha consultation.
  • Panchanga values vary by school, ayanamsa, and location. Classifications here may differ from your family pundit's practice.
  • For dates that matter — marriage, travel, major purchases, ceremonies — consult a qualified jyotishi and confirm against a trusted panchanga.
  • All computation uses the Lahiri ayanamsa (True Chitra Paksha) and the Swiss Ephemeris.
