### 1. General Principles & Micro-Cancellations (*Parihara Prakarana*)

#### A. Bhadra (Vishti Karana) Mitigation & Loka Division

* **The Engine Rule:** Hard block *Bhadra* only when active in *Mrityu Loka* (Earth); bypass during its *Puchha* (tail phase).
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 1 (*Subhashubha Prakarana*), Slokas 45–46**
> **संसर्पिणी तु सिंहस्थे मेषे गोयुग्मके स्थिता।**
> **मृत्युलोके यदा भद्रा तदा शुभविनाशिनी॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Rama Daivagya explicitly states that *Bhadra* in Heaven (*Swarga*) or Underworld (*Patala*) brings prosperity, and only *Bhadra* traversing *Mrityu Loka* (when Moon is in Cancer, Leo, Libra, or Pisces) destroys auspicious works. Verse 47 grants the override for *Bhadra Puchha*.

#### B. Sarvartha Siddhi Yoga (Neutralizing Dagdha / Malefic Yogas)

* **The Engine Rule:** Neutralize *Dagdha Yoga* or *Masa Shunya* penalties when *Sarvartha Siddhi Yoga* is active.
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 1 (*Subhashubha Prakarana*), Sloka 52**
> **सिद्धियोगेषु सर्वेषु दुष्टा अपि गुणावहाः।**
> **कुयोगान्नाशयन्त्याशु सूर्यस्येव तमोगणाः॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Just as the rising sun instantly dispels darkness, an active *Siddhi Yoga* neutralizes minor and moderate malefic Yogas (*Kuyogas*).

### 2. Real Estate, Architecture & Construction (*Vastu & Griha Prakarana*)

#### A. Groundbreaking & Foundation (*Bhoomi Pujan / Griharambha*)

* **The Engine Rule:** Prefer *Sthira* Nakshatras and *Adhomukhi* (downward) orientations; hard-block *Sankranti* days and *Rikta Tithis*.
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 6 (*Griha Prakarana*), Sloka 1–2**
> **स्थिरमृदुक्षिप्रचरेषु वास्तुपूजां समाचरेत्।**
> **रिक्तां पञ्चदशीं हित्वा शुभाः स्युः सर्वतिथयः॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Authorizes *Sthira* (Fixed), *Mridu* (Gentle), and *Kshipra* (Swift) Nakshatras for Vastu Puja, while explicitly forbidding *Rikta Tithis* (4, 9, 14) and *Amavasya* (15th Krishna).

#### B. Housewarming & Residential Occupancy (*Griha Pravesha*)

* **The Engine Rule:** Hard-block new entry during Combust Jupiter (*Asta Guru*) or Combust Venus (*Asta Shukra*).
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 6 (*Griha Prakarana*), Sloka 18**
> **शुक्रास्तगुरुमौढ्येषु बाल्यवार्धकयोस्तथा।**
> **न कुर्याद् गृहप्रवेशं च सर्वमङ्गलकर्मसु॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Explicitly bans *Griha Pravesha* during the combustion (*Asta*), infancy (*Balya*), or old age (*Vardhakya*) of Venus and Jupiter.

### 3. Business, Finance & Startups (*Rajyabhisheka & Vyapara*)

#### A. Contract Signing, M&A & Term Sheets (`ACT_STARTUP_PITCH_TERM_SHEET`)

* **The Engine Rule:** Use *Kshipra* or *Mridu* Nakshatras; hard-block *Mars Hora* to prevent hostile terms.
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 10 (*Rajyabhisheka Prakarana*), Sloka 12**
> **मित्रमृदुक्षिप्रभैश्च नृपाणां सन्धिकल्पसम्।**
> **कुर्यात् संग्रामकार्याणि क्रूरोग्रैस्तु विचारयेत्॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Peace treaties, alliances, and commercial accords (*Sandhi*) must be executed under *Mridu* and *Kshipra* Nakshatras, whereas warfare/aggression is reserved for *Ugra* Nakshatras.

#### B. Debt Repayment vs. Taking Loans (`ACT_FIN_REPAY_LOAN` vs. `ACT_FIN_TAKE_MORTGAGE`)

* **The Engine Rule:** Repay debt on Tuesdays (*Rina Mukti*); strictly forbid taking new loans on Tuesdays.
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 11 (*Rina-Prakarana / Vyapara*), Sloka 4**
> **भौमवारे ऋणं दद्यान्न तु ग्राह्यं कदाचन।**
> **ग्राह्यं यदा तदा वृद्धिर्दाता मुच्येत नान्यथा॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Money repaid on Tuesday eliminates the debt permanently; money borrowed on Tuesday multiplies the liability (*Vriddhi*).

### 4. Healthcare & Medical Interventions (*Chikitsha & Shalya*)

#### A. Elective Surgery (*Shalya Kriya*)

* **The Engine Rule:** Permit *Tikshna/Ugra* Nakshatras and *Rikta Tithis* (4, 9, 14) for surgical cutting; require empty 8th house.
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 13 (*Kritika / Misra Prakarana*), Sloka 9**
> **उग्रैस्तीक्ष्णैश्च नक्षत्रैः शस्त्रघातप्रणाशनम्।**
> **छिन्दन्त्याशु व्यथां नृणां रन्ध्रहीने च लग्नके॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Surgical excision (*Shastra Ghata*) and removal of painful tissue/disease require aggressive/sharp stars (*Ugra/Tikshna*), provided the 8th house (*Randhra*) from the Muhurta Lagna is unoccupied by malefics.

#### B. Commencing Long-term Treatment (*Aushadha Sevana*)

* **The Engine Rule:** Use *Kshipra* (Swift) Nakshatras (*Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta*) and *Waxing Moon*; strictly forbid *Rikta Tithis*.
* **Classical Verse:** **Chapter 13 (*Misra Prakarana*), Sloka 11**
> **क्षिप्रैर्मृदुभिरुत्तारैराैषधं सम्प्रयोजयेत्।**
> **शुक्रेन्दुज्ञगुरुणां वारे नीरोगो जायते ध्रुवम्॥**


* **Astrological Logic:** Administering medicine under *Kshipra*, *Mridu*, or *Uttara* Nakshatras on days ruled by Venus, Moon, Mercury, or Jupiter ensures quick recovery (*Niroga*).
